## 🍑 What is HALCYON // 2000?

**HALCYON // 2000** fuses cozy Tamagotchi-era 16-bit pixel aesthetics with cutting-edge real-time AI computer vision. By running Google MediaPipe Tasks Vision on-device in the browser, your index fingertips transform into magic pastel wands shedding stardust and sparkles.

No harsh black, no neon glare, no intrusive downloads. Just pure, whimsical Y2K bliss running smoothly at **60 FPS** on both desktop and mobile devices.

## ✨ Features

### 🖐️ Dual-Hand MediaPipe AI Computer Vision
- **Simultaneous Dual Slicing:** Tracks up to `2 hands` in real time with continuous line-to-circle swept physics. Slice with both hands at once using dual glowing pastel trails!
- **Zero-Friction Mouse & Touch Fallback:** If camera access is skipped or unavailable, mouse drag and mobile touch work instantly with zero configuration.
- **Bright & Crisp Camera Canvas:** Your live webcam feed is mirrored, gently brightened, and layered with your choice of soft pastel wash overlays and gentle vignettes.

### ✌️ "Peace & Love" Easter Egg (+5 Bonus)
- Hold up a **Peace Sign** (`✌️` — index and middle fingers extended, ring and pinky curled) for 0.5s during gameplay to trigger a celebratory **"PEACE & LOVE +5 ✨"** stardust burst with music-box chime audio and a 10-second cooldown!

### ⏱️ Customizable Duration & Endless Vibe Mode
- **Play Your Way:** Choose from quick `30s`, standard `60s`, `90s`, full-track `3m` (180s), or input any custom timer from `10s` to `600s`.
- **Endless Vibe ♾️ Mode:** Chill without time pressure! The HUD clock counts up as long as you want to vibe.
- **Cozy Bomb Handling:** Slicing a Grumpy Raincloud bomb in Timed or Endless modes triggers a screen shake, deducts `-5` points, and resets your combo streak without cutting your session short. (Classic 3-Hearts life system is always available in Chaos Mode).

### 🪄 Wand & Filter Customizer
- **Magic Wand Palettes:** Switch between `🌈 Pastel Rainbow`, `🌸 Sakura Petals`, or `💚 Cyber Mint` ribbon trails.
- **Camera Filter Presets:** Post-process your video feed in real time with `🍬 Cotton Candy Wash`, `🎮 90s Pink GameBoy`, or `🌿 Natural Soft`.

### 🎧 Persistent "Pick Your Vibe" Music Player
A collapsible Y2K music deck in the top-right corner that **never interrupts** across menus, gameplay, or game over:
1. **📁 Local Audio:** Drag-and-drop or select any `.mp3`, `.wav`, or `.m4a` file with loop, volume, and playback controls.
2. **🔗 Stream Link:** Paste any Spotify or YouTube URL to stream your favorite lo-fi tracks inside a pastel frame.
3. **✦ Built-In Procedural Synthesizers:** Instant multi-oscillator Web Audio chiptune beats:
   - 🌙 *Midnight Synthwave* (110 BPM bassline, Cm7/Fm7 lush pads, retro drums)
   - 🎵 *Seoul Chill R&B* (78 BPM sub-bass, Cmaj7/Am7 soft keys, brushed lo-fi kit)
   - ✦ *Pastel Fairycore* (138 BPM 8-bit cycling arpeggios, staccato bass, happy lead)
- **Audio-Reactive Border:** Real-time Web Audio `AnalyserNode` frequency detection pulses the canvas border to the bass of your music!

### 📸 Y2K Polaroid Photo-Booth & Souvenir Cards
- Finished your run? Tap **"Snap Vibe Polaroid 📸"** for an authentic 3-2-1 countdown snapshot!
- Frames your camera shot (or canvas artwork) inside an authentic white retro Polaroid card complete with decorative pixel hearts, score, peak combo, date stamp, and a generated rank title:
  - 👑 *Legendary Fruit Empress*
  - 🧚 *Certified Fruit Fairy*

  - 🪄 *Combo Wizard*
  - ✨ *Slice Apprentice*
  - 🍰 *Snack Slicer*

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Because HALCYON // 2000 uses modern ES Modules (`type="module"`) and webcam APIs, it must be served over `http://` or `https://` (not `file://`):

### Option 1: Python (pre-installed on macOS/Windows/Linux)
```bash
# Navigate to the project directory
cd pastel-slice-2000

# Start a local static server
python -m http.server 3000
```
Open **`http://localhost:3000`** in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari.

### Option 2: Node.js (npx serve)
```bash
cd pastel-slice-2000
npx serve .
```
